1. Unzip this package.
2. Open the unzipped folder.
3. Select ALL files and the assets folder.
4. In GitHub, choose Add file > Upload files.
5. Drag everything into the repository root.
6. Confirm index.html and assets appear immediately at the top level.
7. Click Commit changes.
8. Cloudflare Pages should redeploy automatically.

Do not upload the ZIP itself.
